The craft

How a hand is read

The four elements

A square palm and short fingers is the earth hand: build, keep, finish. A square palm and long fingers is the air hand: think, explain, design. A long palm and short fingers is the fire hand: start, lead, risk. A long palm and long fingers is the water hand: feel, imagine, counsel. Most people are louder in one element and borrow from a second.

The major lines

The heart line, high on the palm, is the style of love. The head line is how the mind moves, not a test score. The life line around the thumb is vitality and the seasons of a life, never a death date. The fate line, when it exists, is direction and the world having a vote. A missing fate line is not a missing future. It often means you name the work yourself.

Mounts

Venus, at the thumb, is appetite and warmth. Jupiter, under the index, is ambition with a standard. Saturn, under the middle, is weight and time. Apollo, under the ring, is visibility and joy in making. Mercury, under the little finger, is trade, wit, and healing talk. The Moon, on the outer palm, is imagination and the pull to roam.

Three traditions

Western palmistry names heart, head, life, and fate. Vedic hasta samudrika uses the same rivers and adds a stronger reading of duty, mounts, and marks. Chinese hand study is plainer about career, constitution, and emotional clarity. Palmora can speak in one voice or braid the three. The hand does not change when the language does.

A useful photograph

Daylight, not a yellow lamp. Palm flat, all five fingers in frame, thumb slightly open. Fill the picture with the hand. If the scanner cannot lock the fingers, walk the lines yourself. Your eyes are allowed to be the instrument.

What this will not do

It will not tell you when you die, name a disease, or promise a marriage. Small lines that some readers call children or weddings are too easy to invent from a photo, so they are not counted as facts. Use the reading as a serious mirror, then decide for yourself.
