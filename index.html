What can be known from a photo

Why a pretty photo can still be wrong

A palmist feels mount height, skin, and flexibility, and watches a crease from more than one angle. A single picture cannot do that. Palmora measures fingertips and the thumb, because those landmarks are stable. It only drafts the creases. You must accept, reject, or redraw each line. An unchecked line is not read, and it is not called missing.

What a professional still looks for

Order of a real sitting: hand shape, which finger leads, thumb will against thumb reason, then heart, head, life, and fate, then the space between heart and head (the quadrangle) and the great triangle inside life, head, and fate. Only after that: sun line, Mercury, crosses, stars, triangles, islands, and joins. Marks that are not confirmed are omitted.

Money, fame, crosses

Money wants a closed triangle of head, fate, and Mercury, or clear vertical lines under the little finger. Fame wants a sun line that you can see, or a star on Apollo. A cross is two short creases, not every wrinkle. If you cannot see it on the photo, do not tap it.
