The craft

What is allowed to be read

Evidence before story

Palmora names a sign only when the photo traces it, or when you tap it yourself. If the hand is small, dark, or the skin is a net of noise, minor marks are skipped. Absence is interpreted only on a clear photo. A missing line on a bad picture is not a destiny.

Cross, star, triangle, delta

A cross is two short creases in an X. A star has more arms. A triangle is a closed three-sided figure. A delta is a small triangle. On Jupiter they lean toward leadership. On Apollo, a star leans toward visibility and a cross leans toward delay. On Mercury, a triangle leans toward business judgment. On Saturn, a cross or star is a classical caution, not a death mark. A mystic cross sits between the head and heart and is read as perception, not as proof of visions.

Money, fame, success

The money triangle is a closed meeting of the head line, the fate line, and the Mercury line. Vertical lines on Mercury are business lines. Fame wants a real sun line, a star or triangle on Apollo, or a trident. Success in the classical sense wants a star or triangle on Jupiter, a fate line that actually arrives, or a square protecting a break. If those marks are missing, the app says so.

Joins, islands, forks

A fate line that leaves the life line is self-made effort. One that rises from the Moon is shaped by other people. Head tied to life is caution. A wide gap is independence. An island is a split that rejoins: a season, not a life sentence. A chain is many small islands. A fork at the end of the heart, between index and middle, is the balanced heart. A fork on the head is a mind with two tools, and sometimes two aims.

What this app will not count

Hairlines that some readers call children, or a number of marriages, are too often created by a camera. They are not counted. You may tap short horizontal lines under the little finger if you see them. They are read as bonds, never as a number. No sign here dates a death or names a disease.
